## Valuation: CFV & DGSB (valuation-cfv-dgsb.md)

The wiki's own instruction is to teach this the way you would explain how the meter is defined.
Tier C items trap the two misreadings: that the CFV predicts appreciation, and that the DGSB
tracks Bitcoin.
