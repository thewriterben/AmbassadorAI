## Participation pathways (participation-pathways.md, WP §10)

This section is deliberately heavy on Tier C traps, drawn from the wiki's own "what this is NOT"
section. The recruiting misreading is the most damaging thing an ambassador can say, so the
distractors quote it verbatim in order to mark it false.

| Tier | Topic | Question | Correct | Distractors | Explanation | Source |
