## Legal positioning & safe harbor (positioning-safe-harbor.md, WP §12)

The wiki calls "safe harbor" the most misunderstood talking point DGD has. Every item here teaches
the legal meaning and traps the financial misreading.

| Tier | Topic | Question | Correct | Distractors | Explanation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Safe harbor | In DGD materials, "safe harbor" is a term from: | Law and regulation | Investing · Banking · Insurance | It means designed to fall outside securities law. | positioning-safe-harbor.md |
| A | Howey | What does the Howey test decide? | Whether something is an investment contract, and therefore a security | Whether a coin is scarce · Whether a network is decentralised · Whether a wallet is self-custodial | DGD is designed to fall outside it. | glossary.md, WP §12 |
| A | Taxonomy | The March 2026 Joint Interpretive Release splits crypto assets into how many categories? | 5 | 3 · 4 · 7 | Digital commodities, digital collectibles, digital tools, payment stablecoins and digital securities. | WP §12.7 |

| B | Digital commodity | The definition of a digital commodity turns on value coming from the protocol and supply and demand rather than from: | Expectations of profit | Government backing · Exchange listings · Mining difficulty | That exact phrase is the legal test. | WP §12.7 |

| B | Structure | Why does the White Paper argue DGD does not need to rely on the startup or fundraising exemptions? | The founder self-funded development and there was no capital raise, so DGD is a distributor rather than an issuer | Because it is open source · Because supply is capped · Because it uses Proof-of-Stake | It is framed as satisfying the principles outright. | WP §12.8 |

| C | Safe harbor | True or false: "safe harbor" means DGD is a safe investment. | False | True | It is about staying outside securities law, not about anyone's money. Safe harbor is about the law, not your wallet. | positioning-safe-harbor.md |
