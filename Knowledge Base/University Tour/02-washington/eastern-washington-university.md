## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**Chapter 172-137 WAC — Use of University Facilities; Chapter 172-141 WAC — Use of outdoor areas for first amendment activities**

WAC 172-137-050: 'University facilities MAY NOT BE USED FOR COMMERCIAL GAIN, including: Commercial advertising; solicitation and merchandising of any food, goods, wares, service, or merchandise.' ⚠ CRITICAL: 'All requests for the use of university facilities by students, nonaffiliated groups, and outside community members, WHETHER SPONSORED OR NOT, must be approved by the VP for Business & Finance or designee' — RSO sponsorship does NOT bypass this at EWU. Reservations ≥48 hours in advance; fees at actual direct and indirect cost. WAC 172-141: non-affiliates MAY engage in first amendment activities outdoors, 6am–10pm, ≤8 hours/day, ≤5 days per two-week period when contested; activities likely to draw 100+ people require 3 days' notice to university police at 509-359-4021. But 172-137-060 permits distribution of NONCOMMERCIAL materials only.

**Sponsorship requirement:** Sponsorship is irrelevant — VP-BF approval required regardless

Source: <https://apps.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=172-137&full=true>
