## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**Solicitation Policy (Gonzaga University Policies, student conduct standards)**

THE HARDEST 'NO' IN THE PACIFIC NORTHWEST. Verbatim: 'In order to protect student's right to privacy, UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES ARE SOLICITORS (INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL) ALLOWED TO CANVAS THE CAMPUS.' And: 'The use of University facilities and resources to conduct commercial ventures not sanctioned by the University is prohibited.' Enforcement: 'Individuals engaged in these activities will be asked to leave the premises. Failure to do so will result in the staff contacting Campus Security & Public Safety.' The ONLY approval route is the Gonzaga University Event Service Team (GUEST) for a sanctioned vendor table. As a private institution Gonzaga is bound by neither the WAC chapters nor public-forum doctrine.
