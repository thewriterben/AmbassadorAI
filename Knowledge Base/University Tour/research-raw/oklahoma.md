# OKLAHOMA CAMPUS RESEARCH — Fall 2026 DGD Student Outreach Tour

## STATEWIDE LEGAL CONTEXT

**Okla. Stat. tit. 70, § 2120 — "Protected expressive activities on campus."**
Added by **Laws 2019, c. 212, § 1 (SB 361)**; amended by **Laws 2022, c. 18, § 3 (HB 3543)**; further amended 2026 (SB 1725).
- https://law.justia.com/codes/oklahoma/title-70/section-70-2120/
- Statute text on OSCN: https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/DeliverDocument.asp?CiteID=485927 — **robots-blocked to my tooling**; Justia used instead.
- FIRE tracker: https://www.fire.org/research-learn/enacted-campus-free-speech-statutes-oklahoma

**Two carve-outs kill this statute as a tool for DGD:**

1. **Commercial carve-out.** Subsection D opens: *"Any person who wishes to engage in **noncommercial** expressive activity on campus shall be permitted to do so freely…"* A crypto project promoting a product is commercial speech and sits outside the protected class.
2. **Standing carve-out.** *"'Campus community' means students, administrators, faculty and staff at the public institution of higher education **and their invited guests**."* Subsection C: *"The outdoor areas of campuses of public institutions of higher education in this state shall be deemed public forums **for the campus community**, and public institutions of higher education shall not create 'free speech zones' or other designated areas of campus outside of which expressive activities are prohibited."* The public-forum designation runs to the campus community, not to the general public. An unaffiliated commercial entity only gets in as an **invited guest** of a student, faculty member or RSO — which is exactly what the sponsorship/anti-fronting rules below then police.

**Practical read:** the free-speech-zone ban is real and useful for *students* handing out DGD literature. It gives DGD-the-company nothing. Every Oklahoma public campus can lawfully price, permit, or refuse a commercial table — and OSU and OU both do, with published rates.

**Also on the books, higher-ed:** **70 O.S. § 2119.1 — "Protection for religious student associations"** (Laws 2014, c. 350, § 2, eff. Nov. 1, 2014): *"No public institution of higher education may take any action or enforce any policy that would deny a religious student association any benefit available to any other student association…"* Irrelevant to DGD except as evidence Oklahoma legislates aggressively on RSO parity. https://law.justia.com/codes/oklahoma/2014/title-70/section-70-2119.1/

**On the "Oklahoma Students' Religious Liberties Act":** I could **not** confirm its codified section. 70 O.S. § 11-105.1 is sex-education curriculum; § 11-106 is parental inspection of instructional materials — neither is it. The Act (2013, HB 1372) is a **K-12 public-school-district** statute and does **not** govern higher education. **Do not cite it in campus outreach materials.** The higher-ed statutes are § 2120 and § 2119.1. Verify at https://law.justia.com/codes/oklahoma/title-70/

**Statewide policy layer:** Oklahoma State Regents for Higher Education, **Freedom of Expression policy** — https://okhighered.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/freedom-of-expression-policy.pdf. It is a Chicago-Statement-style declaration ("*A college or university may reasonably regulate the time, place, and manner of expression, to ensure that it does not disrupt the ordinary activities of the institution*"). It contains **no commercial-speech provision and no provision addressing non-affiliated persons** — it neither helps nor hurts DGD.

**All six campuses are on SEMESTERS. No quarter, block, trimester or quad school in this set.**

## C. Solicitation / Outside-Vendor Policy
**OU Facility Use Policies (Campus Scheduling)** — https://www.ou.edu/scheduling/policies — this is the operative document. Quoted verbatim:

**Solicitation:**
> "Unsolicited sales door-to-door, office-to-office, or in open areas, by commercial groups or individuals for commercial or financial gain is not allowed."

> "Solicitation shall be prohibited on campus except for solicitation by university departments, RSOs or branches of the SGA which may occur in conjunction with regular student activities and campus events."

RSOs must file a **Solicitation Request three (3) working days prior** and pay solicitation fees for indoor/outdoor fundraising.

**Anti-fronting — this is the decisive clause for DGD:**
> "'Fronting' is defined as permitting a non-University individual or organization to use University space/facilities and services under the guise that the activity is a University-sponsored program in order to receive a discounted rate or avoid payment. Fronting is prohibited by University policy."

If discovered, non-university rental rates apply and the RSO/students may be referred to the Office of Student Conduct.

**Does sponsorship cure it? No — sponsorship is flatly barred:**
> "Student organizations and University departments may not sponsor Non-University groups, contractors, vendors, or organizations."

**Co-sponsorship is the only channel, and it is narrow:**
> "University departments and RSOs may co-sponsor events with off-campus organizations in accordance with the following guidelines: The event or meeting must be planned and managed by a university department and/or RSOs."
Plus: a majority of attendees must be OU-affiliated, and *"the mission of the outside group relates to the on-campus group."*

**Third-party definition and insurance:**
> "Third party groups are outside entities not affiliated with the University that have been hired and/or contracted to provide a service or to conduct business on campus. These groups do not fall under any insurance coverage or self-insurance provisions maintained by the University or the State of Oklahoma and therefore must acquire their own liability coverage and any other insurance as necessary for risks associated with a Special Event."
Proof of liability insurance to Risk Management **at least 5 working days prior**. No dollar limit published on this page.

**Free expression:**
> "The outdoor areas of campus that are generally accessible to the public are available to be used for expressive activity on a first-come, first-served basis."
> "The University does not limit demonstration activity taking place along its public roads and sidewalks, including leafleting and the dissemination of information, if the activity does not unreasonably restrict pedestrian traffic, cause damage to landscaping, disrupt the normal operations of the University, or otherwise violate law or University policy."
Note the tension: leafleting on public sidewalks is open, but *commercial* solicitation in open areas is prohibited by the clause above. The distinction OU draws is non-commercial expression vs. sales.

**Payment credentials — direct hit on section C's payment-app question:**

## 4. The state's campus free-speech statute

**Okla. Stat. tit. 70, § 2120 — "Protected expressive activities on campus."**
- **Enacted:** Laws 2019, c. 212, § 1 — **SB 361 (2019)**.
- **Amended:** Laws 2022, c. 18, § 3 — **HB 3543 (2022)**, which also added §§ 3205.11–3205.14 creating the **Oklahoma Free Speech Committee** within the State Regents for Higher Education, requiring published complaint procedures and First Amendment training for administrators.
- **Further amended: SB 1725 (2026)** — prohibits content-based security fees for events, clarifies the harassment standard, and mandates student free-speech training covering public forums and content-neutral restrictions.
- **Applies to:** public institutions of higher education and technology centers. **Not to TU or OCU.**
- **What it does:** bars "free speech zones"; deems outdoor campus areas public forums for the campus community; permits only content- and viewpoint-neutral, narrowly tailored time/place/manner rules; adopts the *Davis v. Monroe County Bd. of Educ.* harassment standard; requires free-speech education at orientation and annual reporting; **creates a private cause of action.**

**The two clauses that matter to DGD, verbatim:**
> **§ 2120(D):** *"Any person who wishes to engage in **noncommercial** expressive activity on campus shall be permitted to do so freely…"*

> **Definition:** *"'Campus community' means students, administrators, faculty and staff at the public institution of higher education **and their invited guests**."*

> **§ 2120(C):** *"The outdoor areas of campuses of public institutions of higher education in this state shall be deemed public forums for the campus community, and public institutions of higher education shall not create 'free speech zones' or other designated areas of campus outside of which expressive activities are prohibited."*

**Bottom line: commercial speech is carved out, and the forum right runs to the campus community, not the public.** OU's $300/day fee, OSU's $250 permit and $400/day table, and every campus's approval requirement are all lawful under this statute. **Do not let an ambassador cite § 2120 as a right to table. It is not one.**

**Companion higher-ed statute:** **70 O.S. § 2119.1**, "Protection for religious student associations" (Laws 2014, c. 350, § 2, eff. Nov. 1, 2014) — *"No public institution of higher education may take any action or enforce any policy that would deny a religious student association any benefit available to any other student association…"* Irrelevant to DGD directly, but evidence Oklahoma legislates on RSO parity.

**⚠ CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF:** the **"Oklahoma Students' Religious Liberties Act"** is a **K-12 public-school-district statute (2013, HB 1372)** and does **not** govern higher education. I checked 70 O.S. § 11-105.1 (sex-education curriculum) and § 11-106 (parental inspection of instructional materials) — **neither is the Act**, and I could not confirm its codified section. **It is not a campus free-speech statute and must not appear in DGD materials.** The higher-ed statutes are § 2120 and § 2119.1.

**Statewide policy layer (not a statute):** OSRHE **Freedom of Expression policy** — https://okhighered.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/freedom-of-expression-policy.pdf. Chicago-Statement style. Permits reasonable time/place/manner regulation. **Contains no commercial-speech provision and no provision on non-affiliated persons.** Neutral for DGD.

**Institution-level implementations:** OSU/A&M **Board of Regents Policy 3.13**, "Extracurricular Use of Institutional Facilities, Areas or Media for the Purpose of Expression," amended June 22, 2018 and Sept. 13, 2019 — https://regents.okstate.edu/node/287. OSU free-speech hub with complaint form and annual report: https://studentaffairs.okstate.edu/free-speech/
