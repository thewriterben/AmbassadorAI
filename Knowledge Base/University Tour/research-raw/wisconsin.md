## STATE-LEVEL FINDINGS — read before any campus section

### The operative rule is UWS 18.11(8), NOT UWS 21

The brief predicted Wis. Admin. Code ch. **UWS 21 "Use of University Facilities"** would answer the
access question systemwide. It half does. UWS 21 was retrieved and confirmed
(https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/code/admin_code/uws/21 — chapter page fetched successfully;
**all subsection-level URLs under /uws/21/... and the whole of /uws/18 are ROBOTS-BLOCKED** to
research tooling, and direct curl to docs.legis.wisconsin.gov returns a proxy 403).

**UWS 21.01 Declaration of policy:** "It is the policy of the Board of Regents that the facilities
of the university are to be used primarily for purposes of fulfilling the university's missions of
teaching, research and public service."

**UWS 21.04(1) Use by persons or organizations not associated with an institution:** "The chancellor
of each institution, or his or her designee, may permit persons, or organizations not associated
with that institution, to use university facilities" if:
- (a) "The proposed use is under the sponsorship or at the invitation of an organization associated
  with the institution"
- (b) "The proposed use will not interfere with or detract from the teaching, research and public
  service missions of the institution, or the use of the facilities by organizations associated with
  the institution"
- (c) "The institution has appropriate facilities available for the proposed use"
- (d) "The person or organization has complied with institutional procedures adopted under s. UWS 21.06"

**UWS 21.04(2):** "Persons or organizations using university facilities under this section must
reimburse the institutions for the costs, if any, incident to the use of the facilities."

**UWS 21.06:** chancellors must publish (1) procedures for securing permission, (2) "A schedule of
the costs and rental fees, if any, assessed for the use of facilities," and (3) time/manner limits.

Created Register, December 1986, No. 372, effective 1-1-87.

**⚠ UWS 21 CONTAINS NO COMMERCIAL-ACTIVITY CLAUSE.** The commercial provision is in a different
chapter, and it is the sentence an ambassador will actually be quoted at:

**Wis. Admin. Code UWS 18.11(8) "SELLING, PEDDLING AND SOLICITING"** (ch. UWS 18, Conduct on
University Lands — retrieved via the Justia mirror,
https://regulations.justia.com/states/wisconsin/uws/chapter-uws-18/section-uws-18-11/, because the
Legislature's own page for ch. 18 is robots-blocked):

> "No person may sell, peddle or solicit for the sale of goods, services, or contributions on any
> university lands except in the case of:
> (a) Specific permission in advance from a specific university office or the occupant of a
> university house, apartment, or residence hall;
> (b) Sales by an individual of personal property owned or acquired by the seller primarily for
> his/her own use pursuant to an allocation;
> (c) Sales of newspapers and similar printed matter outside university buildings;
> (d) Subscription, membership, ticket sales solicitation, fund-raising, selling, and soliciting
> activities by or under the sponsorship of a university or registered student organization;
> (e) Admission events in a university building pursuant to contract with the university, and food,
> beverage or other concessions conducted pursuant to a contract;
> (f) Solicitation of political contributions under ch. 11, Stats., and institutional regulations
> governing time, place and manner."

**This one subsection answers the access question for eight of the nine campuses.** Two doors exist
and both are written into the code:
- **18.11(8)(a)** — buy permission from a named office. UW Oshkosh has priced it ($75 concourse
  table for "Other individuals/groups"); UW-La Crosse has priced it and even discounts it;
  UW-Eau Claire says a fee will be charged but does not publish the number.
- **18.11(8)(d)** — act "**by or under the sponsorship of** a university or registered student
  organization." Unlike Oklahoma, where OU affirmatively prohibits sponsorship, **the Wisconsin
  code expressly blesses the sponsorship route.** Campus rules narrow it (UWM confines it to the
  Union building; UW-Madison bars a corporate sponsor from using an RSO's fair table), but nowhere
  in the public system is sponsorship forbidden outright.

### There is NO Wisconsin campus free-speech statute

The brief asked whether one exists. **It does not.** 2025 Senate Bill 498 (Campus Free Speech and
Academic Freedom) was introduced Oct 21, 2025 and **passed the legislature Feb 12, 2026, 53-45 on
party lines**; Governor Evers stated on Jan 6, 2026 that he would veto it, and no enactment could
be found. Sources:
https://campus-speech.law.duke.edu/campus-speech-incidents/wisconsin-senate-bill-498-2025-campus-free-speech-and-academic-freedom/
· https://www.wispolitics.com/2026/assembly-sends-campus-free-speech-bill-to-evers/ (paywalled)
· https://www.dailycardinal.com/article/2026/01/evers-says-he-would-veto-republican-led-campus-free-speech-bill

Earlier attempts died too: AB 299 (2017), AB 444 (2019), AB 553 (2023).

The governing layer is therefore **Regent Policy Document 4-21, "Commitment to Academic Freedom and
Freedom of Expression," adopted October 6, 2017 (Resolution 10952)** —
https://www.wisconsin.edu/regents/policies/commitment-to-academic-freedom-and-freedom-of-expression/
It applies to "all UW System students, employees, and **visitors**"; it permits institutions to
"reasonably regulate the time, place, and manner of expression" and imposes only "reasonable
viewpoint-neutral and content-neutral restrictions." **It contains NO commercial-speech provision
and does not designate campus a public forum.** It is neutral for DGD and cannot be cited as a right
to table.

**⚠ DO NOT LET AN AMBASSADOR CLAIM A WISCONSIN CAMPUS FREE-SPEECH LAW.** Wisconsin is not Oklahoma
or Texas. Claiming a statute that a veto killed six months ago destroys credibility instantly.

### State posture on digital assets

The **State of Wisconsin Investment Board bought $164 million of two spot bitcoin ETFs in early
2024** — the first US state pension fund to do so. UW-Whitewater's Paul Nylen was WPR's on-record
expert, calling the state "a little under-invested" (allocation under 1%, he suggested 1–3%).
https://www.wpr.org/news/wisconsin-invest-bitcoin-efts-uw-whitewater-professor (May 24, 2024).
This is the single most useful conversational opener in the state and it is verifiably true.

### Geography

Madison–Milwaukee 80 miles. Whitewater sits between them (45 min from Madison, 50 from Milwaukee) —
**Madison + Whitewater + Milwaukee is a genuine three-stop, two-day corridor.** Oshkosh and Appleton
(Lawrence) are 20 miles apart and ~90 min north of Milwaukee — a second natural pair. **Eau Claire,
La Crosse and Menomonie (Stout) are a separate western trip**: Eau Claire–Menomonie is 25 miles,
Eau Claire–La Crosse 90 miles, and La Crosse is 3.5 hours from Madison. Treat the state as three
trips, not one.

### Calendar shape

All eight public campuses are on **semesters**. **Lawrence runs TRIMESTERS** — three 10-week terms,
three courses at a time — and its Fall Term is over on **November 24**, three-plus weeks before
anyone else. Start dates cluster hard: Marquette Aug 31; Madison, Milwaukee, Eau Claire, Whitewater
and Stout all Sep 2; La Crosse Sep 8; Oshkosh Sep 9; Lawrence Sep 14. **Only Marquette starts in
August. Nine campuses inside a fifteen-day window makes the first two weeks of September the whole
ballgame.**

## 4. The state campus free-speech statute

**THERE IS NONE.** 2025 SB 498 passed the legislature Feb 12, 2026 and Governor Evers had already
stated on Jan 6, 2026 that he would veto it. AB 299 (2017), AB 444 (2019) and AB 553 (2023) all died
earlier. The governing layer is **Regent Policy Document 4-21, "Commitment to Academic Freedom and
Freedom of Expression," adopted Oct 6, 2017 (Resolution 10952)**, which reaches "students, employees,
and **visitors**" but contains **no commercial-speech provision** and permits "reasonable
viewpoint-neutral and content-neutral restrictions on time, place, and manner." **⚠ An ambassador
who cites "Wisconsin's campus free speech law" is citing a vetoed bill and will be corrected on the
spot.** The real law is Wis. Admin. Code **UWS 18.11(8)**, which forbids selling and soliciting on
university lands unless you have advance permission from a named office or are acting under the
sponsorship of a registered student organization. Marquette and Lawrence are private and bound by
none of it.
