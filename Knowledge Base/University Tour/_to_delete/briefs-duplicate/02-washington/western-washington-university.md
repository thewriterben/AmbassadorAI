## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**Chapter 516-36 WAC — Use of University Property; POL-U1000.06 Scheduling of Facilities by Off-Campus Groups**

WAC 516-36-040: 'University property may not be used for private or commercial gain, including: Commercial advertising; solicitation and merchandising' — with limited exceptions requiring PRESIDENTIAL APPROVAL or formal agreements. The paid Info Fair booth operates under that exception hook. WAC 516-36-030: 'Handbills, leaflets, pamphlets, flyers, and similar materials may be distributed ONLY IN RELATION TO UNIVERSITY SANCTIONED ACTIVITIES.' POL-U1000.06: written request to the president or designee, evaluated on connection to university programs, mission compatibility, community-relationship impact, and 'the applicant's qualifications, experience, and FINANCIAL STABILITY.' The university may require an advance deposit, bond or insurance.

**Sponsorship requirement:** No — but invitation + presidential-exception route

Source: <https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=516-36&full=true>
