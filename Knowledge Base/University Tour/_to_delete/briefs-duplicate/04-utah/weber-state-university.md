> ⚠ READ THE 'NO CONTRACTS ON SITE' RULE BEFORE BOOKING. Weber sells for-profit booths at $375, which is clean and unambiguous — but the policy explicitly forbids having students sign any contract for services at the booth. If your validation flow requires accepting terms of service on a phone at the table, that flow is non-compliant at Weber. Redesign the booth as lead-capture-only (collect an email, complete signup later off-campus) or don't book. Aug 28 also falls before a strict Sept–Dec window; flex four days to catch it.
