## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**Policy 4.114 — Use of LC State Campus Spaces (updated Apr 2025); Policy 1.106 — Free Speech (Dec 11, 2025)**

MOST PERMISSIVE PUBLIC CAMPUS IN IDAHO. 'Fundraising and solicitation IS PERMITTED in outdoor public areas' so long as it does not impede normal campus functions. Outside individuals and organizations may rent spaces at published rates. Tabling requires a reservation at least 48 HOURS in advance through Events & Conferences, first come first served. Picketing, petitions and literature distribution permitted in public areas. A public bulletin board in the Student Union Building is designated for community activities.

**Sponsorship requirement:** No — reservation + fee

Source: <https://www.lcsc.edu/media/3p1jnchi/4114-use-of-lc-state-campus-spaces_clean-copy.pdf>
