## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**APM 20.12 — Solicitation and Selling (eff. Aug 3, 2005)**

'Solicitation or canvassing by individuals or organizations on UI property is prohibited without prior approval.' Approval authority: the president or designee, IN WRITING. Broadly prohibits sale of merchandise/services of any kind except as approved. Permission 'does not imply university endorsement.' No RSO-sponsorship requirement stated — written approval is the single gate. Policy does NOT name credit cards or financial products.
