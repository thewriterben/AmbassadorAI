## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**Freedom of Expression and Campus Facilities Policy (eff. Sept 2025), in CSI Campus Operational Policies; College Endorsements Policy (eff. May 2024)**

EXPLICIT COMMERCIAL CARVE-OUT — the clearest 'no' in Idaho. Outdoor areas are open to the public for Protected Expressive Activity, BUT: 'Fundraising, solicitation of donations, and COMMERCIAL ACTIVITIES INVOLVING ADVERTISING, MARKETING, AND SALES ARE NOT CONSIDERED PROTECTED EXPRESSIVE ACTIVITY.' Access must be negotiated separately, not claimed as a right. Reservable outdoor areas: Student Union Patio, Fine Arts Patio, Rose Garden (max 5 consecutive days/month per party). Separately, ANY implication of CSI endorsement requires pre-approval from the OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT.

**Sponsorship requirement:** Negotiated access only; endorsement implications need presidential pre-approval

Source: <https://www.csi.edu/_files/pdf/policies/csi-campus-operational-policies.pdf>
