## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**University Policy 1160 — Solicitation (eff. Aug 2, 2022); Policy 1100 — Use of University Space**

Generally prohibits solicitation and sales inside University Structures. Outside groups may solicit ONLY if 'sponsored by a University unit or University-recognized student organization,' and the student org needs pre-approval to sponsor a non-affiliate. Commercial-purpose publicity needs Student Involvement AND University Advancement sign-off. Literature may be freely distributed in outdoor Public Areas with no reservation (Policy 1100). Raffles prohibited (Idaho gambling law, up to $10,000 fine).

**Sponsorship requirement:** Yes — university unit or recognized RSO

Source: <https://www.boisestate.edu/policy/governance-legal/solicitation/>
