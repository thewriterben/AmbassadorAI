## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**Policy 6.08.00 — Time, Place, Manner for Exercising Freedom of Speech; Policy 6.02.00 — Non-Employee Use of Facilities**

6.08.00 applies to 'members of NIC and the public.' CONCRETE SITING RULE: 'Speech activities are to be conducted no closer than 20 FEET from any exit, entrance, staircase, parking lot or roadway.' Reservation not mandatory but 48-hour notice to Campus Safety encouraged. 6.02.00: 'The college PROHIBITS ENDORSEMENTS of external organizations,' rental rates set by contractual agreement, and — critically — 'All public requests… for the purpose of offering education or training activities' must be routed through the WORKFORCE TRAINING OFFICE. A 'crypto education' pitch will land there. 6.08.00 does not address commercial speech or solicitation — those may be in downloadable procedure docs; verify.

**Sponsorship requirement:** No, but education/training offerings must route through Workforce Training
