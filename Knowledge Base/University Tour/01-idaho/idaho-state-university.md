## C. Solicitation / outside-vendor policy

**ISUPP 11010 — Use of University Space for Expressive Activity (eff. Nov 6, 2024); Student Union Scheduling policies**

Outdoor areas generally available for expressive activity WITHOUT reservation — and the policy covers 'visitors and the public,' not just affiliates. BUT: 'Commercial activity for the profit of any person or business' is prohibited unless approved IN ADVANCE IN WRITING BY THE VICE PRESIDENT over the applicable space. Outside groups must additionally show a 'direct benefit to the students of Idaho State University,' sign a Facility Use Agreement, and pay a 50% NON-REFUNDABLE deposit within 48 hours. For-profit entities receive NO discount (non-profits and government get 25%).

**Sponsorship requirement:** No sponsor, but advance written VP approval + Facility Use Agreement + 50% deposit

Source: <https://www.isu.edu/media/libraries/isu-policies-and-procedures/facilities-and-operations/Use-of-University-Space-for-Expressive-Activity-ISUPP-11010.pdf>
