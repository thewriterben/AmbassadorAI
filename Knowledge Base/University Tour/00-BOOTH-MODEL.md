# The Compliant Booth Model

*One booth design that satisfies the strictest written rule found at any of the 37 campuses.*

This is not a conservative reading. It is the literal intersection of four real policies:

- **Montana State (Catapalooza):** *"Vendors are prohibited from seeking a monetary exchange for products, services, or donations"* — but expressly permits *"informational items, coupons, hand-outs, freebies, company contact information"* and collecting contact info for post-event follow-up.
- **Weber State (Block Party):** *"Vendors may not have students sign any kind of contract for services on site."*
- **Colorado State (LSC):** *"No financial transactions (including requesting credit card information, Venmo or similar app info, etc) are permitted on the Plaza."*
- **WSU (WAC 504-34-140):** distribution *"by means of accosting, confronting, detaining, or waylaying individuals or by hawking is prohibited."*

Designed this way, the same booth works everywhere — and it removes the CARD Act pattern-match (*a tangible inducement offered to a student, on campus, to open a financial account*) that will otherwise alarm every dean of students who sees it.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Age-gate at 18 before anything else** | Colorado and Oregon minors provisions are keyed to ACTUAL KNOWLEDGE. A rep who can see they're talking to a high schooler creates knowledge the company then has. |
| 2 | **Educate at the table; do not transact at the table** | MSU Catapalooza expressly permits 'informational items, coupons, hand-outs, freebies, company contact information' and collecting contact info for post-event follow-up — and expressly bans monetary exchange. Weber State separately bans having students 'sign any kind of contract for services on site.' CSU bans 'requesting credit card information, Venmo or similar app info.' The intersection of these rules IS your national booth policy. |
| 3 | **Capture an email; complete validation off-campus, off-clock** | This single design choice makes the booth compliant at MSU, Weber, CSU and everywhere stricter, and removes the CARD Act pattern-match that alarms administrators. |
| 4 | **Re-confirm interest after the event before any transaction — and never add anyone to a mailing list** | Montana State's Catapalooza rules, verbatim: "If you collect contact information from potential customers interested in purchasing goods or services, you must contact the potential customer after Catapalooza re-confirming their interest before any transactions take place" and "do not add a potential customer to any mailing list without their specific consent." This is the strictest lead-handling rule found, so it sets the national standard: one follow-up that re-confirms interest, then nothing unless they opt in. |
| 5 | **No government ID scanning at a folding table** | If DGD is not an MSB, there is no BSA obligation compelling it — so it is pure liability with no offsetting benefit. |
| 6 | **No raffles anywhere** | Prohibited at Boise State (Idaho gambling law, up to $10,000), university-wide at UArizona, and for ASU departments per an Arizona AG determination. |
| 7 | **Ambassadors disclose the material connection every time** | FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 C.F.R. Part 255. Make it a contract term with a clawback and provide pre-written copy. |
| 8 | **Never say 'free money' or 'risk-free,' never forecast token value** | FTC Act §5 deception risk, and the fastest route to a campus complaint. |
| 9 | **Stay behind the table; no approaching passers-by** | WSU: distribution 'by means of accosting, confronting, detaining, or waylaying individuals or by hawking is prohibited.' UW Wyoming: 'All the members of your group must stay behind the table.' Weber: no 'aggressive sales tactics.' |
| 10 | **Bring the written approval to the table** | Most policies make the individual staffing the booth the person asked to leave. UArizona: 'Unapproved vendors are prohibited on campus' — verify approval status by calling (520) 626-2630. |

## Insurance requirements found

| Campus | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **University of Oregon** | $1M Commercial General Liability / $2M aggregate, UO named as additional insured. TULIP available. |
| **UNLV** | Combined single limits of liability of at least $1,000,000, naming the Board of Regents as additional insured, plus hold-harmless/indemnification. |
| **Colorado School of Mines** | $1M per accident bodily injury; $3M per accident property damage. URMIA-TULIP available. |
| **Western Washington** | May require an advance deposit, bond, or insurance (POL-U1000.06). |
| **University of Arizona** | Certificate required ONLY for vendors providing food/beverage samples or displaying a vehicle — DGD would likely be exempt. |
| **ASU** | Events involving non-university individuals require ≥2 weeks' notice due to added approvals, INSURANCE and fees. |
