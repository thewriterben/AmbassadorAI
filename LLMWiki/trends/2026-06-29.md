# AI Video Trend Radar — 2026-06-29

Weekly, compliance-filtered scan of AI video for DGD ambassadors. Ride trends **educationally** — borrow structure and style, never "buy / pump / safe-investment" framing. Mechanism, not forecast. (Re-confirm free-tier facts on each tool's own site; they change weekly.)

## 🎬 Model & tool watch
The big shift: **native synced audio is now standard** — ~4 of 6 major models generate sound with video, up from zero a year ago. **Veo 3.1** leads on prompt adherence, native audio, and 4K. **Kling 3.0** adds a multi-shot **storyboard mode** with audio sync across cuts, plus *Elements 3.0* to lock characters/props and cut drift. **Runway Gen-4/4.5** stays the control pick (camera moves, motion brush, reference consistency). Note: **Sora's web/app are being discontinued** (API to follow later in 2026) — don't build a workflow on it.

Free-tier reality (verify before relying): **PixVerse** (~90 credits/day) and **Pika** (older 1.x) are the only *no-watermark* free options. **Kling** (~6 clips/day) and **Hailuo** (~200 signup credits) are watermarked at 480p; **Runway** free = 125 one-time credits, no Gen-4; **Luma** ~30/month. Realistic native audio/avatars now trip platform **AI-content labels** — keep them ON.

## 📈 Format & hook trends
The decision window is **~1.5s** (TikTok); algorithms gate on **intro retention** past second ~3, then **completion + replays**. What's *fading*: heavy transitions, text-wall listicles, and aggressive sales hooks. What's *winning*: authentic, one-idea storytelling and longer watch sessions. Benchmarks: a 30s Short at 85% completion beats a 60s at 50%; TikTok sweet spot 15–30s, Reels 30–60s. Translation for us: **open on the surprising fact, not "today I'll explain…".**

## 🎨 Style watch
Captions have **slowed down**: 2–4 word chunks held 600–900ms, with a single **keyword colour-pop** — adopt as **gold/amber keyword pops on deep navy/charcoal, white body**. Also trending: oversized centered hooks in heavy sans, marker-style annotation layers, mixed-case bold over all-caps, and an **analog revival** (film grain, warm tungsten, VHS texture). Use the analog look for "history of money" b-roll in amber+navy — abstract/archival-feeling, never fabricated real footage. Skip the viral Pixar/Miyazaki render fads (copyright risk); keep our clean editorial, illustrative identity.
