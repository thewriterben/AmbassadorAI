# AI Video Trend Radar — 2026-07-03

Weekly, compliance-filtered scan of AI video for DGD ambassadors. Ride trends **educationally** — mechanism, not forecast. Re-confirm free-tier facts on each tool's site; they change weekly.

## 🎬 Model & tool watch
The leaderboard has flipped Chinese: ByteDance's **Seedance 2.0** (Feb) and Alibaba's **HappyHorse 1.0/1.1** (Apr) now top quality rankings, ahead of Veo 3.1 and Runway Gen-4.5. **Seedance 2.5** was unveiled at Volcano Engine Force 2026 and is expected to roll out **this month**: 30-second native generation, up to 50 reference assets, stronger editing control. Free-tier reality is *unstable*: **Hailuo's** free watermark policy flip-flopped between March and April reviews (~3–5 gens/day now); PixVerse and Pika 1.x remain the usual no-watermark picks but **test your own export before relying on it**. Note for EU-reachable content: **EU AI Act Art. 50 requires machine-readable AI markings from Aug 2, 2026** — keep platform AI labels ON regardless.

## 📈 Format & hook trends
Two real shifts: (1) **YouTube Shorts now ranks on watch time per impression**, not swipe-past rate; and (2) all three platforms are **rewarding 60–90s content** over 7–15s clips (more ad inventory). TikTok still weights completion + replays; **Instagram weights saves and shares** (DM-shareable = distribution). Platforms are also detecting and favoring **platform-native** uploads over identical cross-posts. Fading: generic POV hooks, text-wall listicles, aggressive sales hooks. Winning: authentic one-idea storytelling with a real payoff that *earns* the longer runtime.

## 🎨 Style watch
The pendulum keeps swinging **away from over-editing**: clean cuts, balanced color, simple transitions, message-first. **Cinematic calm** — slow zooms, deliberate grading, film-like lighting — is rising in explainer content, and the **analog/VHS grain revival** continues. Captions stay essential (sound-off viewing dominates); keep our slowed 2–4 word chunks with a **gold keyword pop on deep navy/charcoal, white body**. All of this plays directly into the DGD identity: clean editorial, abstract/illustrative b-roll, "boring on purpose." Skip the CapCut glitch/flash-transition template look — it reads as ad, not education.

## ✅ Compliance filter notes

- **Don't chase:** hyper-real people via Seedance/HappyHorse lip-sync — no realistic AI footage of real persons, ever. Use illustrative/abstract avatars, label AI content.
- **Watermark roulette:** Hailuo's free-tier watermark changed twice this spring — verify your own export; a surprise watermark ≠ a disclosure label.
- **Labels stack:** AI-content label + FTC sponsorship disclosure (spoken early + on-screen + front-of-caption) are separate; use both. "Not financial advice" stays in.
- June CPI lands **July 14** — don't pre-state June numbers; May's 4.2% is the current citable figure.
