# Verification Report — 2026-06-29

Weekly re-verification of time-sensitive facts: free AI-tool tiers and social-platform policies. Searches run the week of 2026-06-29. **Net result: 2 material facts changed**, both worth ambassadors' attention. The single most important update this week: **X reversed its 2024 crypto-promotion ban (March 2026)** — paid crypto promotion is now allowed under a Paid Partnership label in most regions but **banned outright in the EU, UK, and Australia**. For DGD this changes nothing operationally (we stay educational, never promotional), but the page was strengthened so ambassadors don't mistake "X now allows it" for "we now do it." Second: **Pika and Pixverse both started watermarking their free exports in 2026**, so the list of clean free AI-video options has shrunk again.

## What I checked
- **Free-tier tools:** Google Veo / Gemini / Flow / Vids, Kling, Hailuo, Pika, Pixverse, ElevenLabs, CapCut, Bing Image Creator, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo, YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, Uppbeat — credits, watermark, resolution, commercial-use.
- **Platform policies (2026 changes):** TikTok, Instagram/Meta, YouTube, X — crypto/financial promotion, branded-content disclosure, AI-generated-content labeling.

## What changed (old → new)

### Tools
1. **Pika & Pixverse — free-tier watermarks (video-generation.md) — TOOL CHANGE THIS WEEK.**
   - *Old:* Both listed as **"No watermark on free"** (Pixverse called "one of the few clean free exports," Pika "exports clean on free").
   - *New:* **Both now watermark free exports.** **Pika** free = ~80 credits/mo, **480p**, with a small semi-transparent Pika logo (clean export now requires paid). **Pixverse** free = ~90 starter + ~60 daily credits, with a **corner watermark and lower resolution** on free. Updated the comparison table and rewrote the "want a watermark-free export?" guidance.
   - *Impact:* The reliable watermark-free *free* AI-video routes have narrowed to **Google Vids (for Veo output)** and **Vheer / Wan** (quality varies). For everything else, plan to **crop/cover** the mark before cross-posting, or upgrade. A stray watermark can get a clip down-ranked on other platforms.

### Platform policies (compliance pages strengthened, never weakened)
2. **X / Twitter — crypto-promotion ban reversed (platform-policies.md) — HEADLINE POLICY CHANGE.**
   - *Old:* X section covered Smart Cashtags + paid-partnership labels; treated crypto promo as "⚠️ avoid" on discipline grounds only.
   - *New:* In **March 2026 X lifted its 2024 crypto-promotion ban** and now permits **paid crypto promotional content under its Paid Partnership framework — but only where local law allows. Crypto promotion remains banned outright in the EU, UK, and Australia.** Added an explicit paragraph stating this does **not** loosen anything for DGD: the Foundation's communications discipline forbids promotional/investment framing everywhere, and EU/UK/AU ambassadors face a hard platform-level ban regardless of framing. Education-not-promotion remains the only globally safe lane.
   - *Impact:* Ambassadors may now *see* paid crypto promotion on X and assume it's fair game. It isn't for us. The strengthened page closes that gap and flags the EU/UK/AU prohibition specifically.

## New risks & opportunities for ambassadors
- **Risk — "X allows crypto promo now" is a trap.** The platform reversal does **not** change DGD discipline, and in the **EU/UK/AU it's banned outright**. Keep every post educational; use the Paid Partnership label only to disclose your connection, never to promote DGD as an investment.
- **Risk — clean free AI-video exports keep shrinking.** With Pika and Pixverse now watermarking free output, the dependable no-watermark free routes are down to **Google Vids and Vheer/Wan**. Preview-check every export for a watermark before cross-posting.
- **Risk unchanged — auto-labeling means you can't skip AI disclosure.** TikTok/YouTube/Meta will label your realistic AI content for you if you don't; being caught not self-disclosing is worse than the label. Self-label every time.
- **Opportunity unchanged — disclosure is genuinely free.** AI/altered labels don't reduce reach or monetization, so honest labeling carries no cost and builds the credibility financial-adjacent education depends on.
- **Watch item — EU AI Act goes fully applicable in ~5 weeks (2 Aug 2026).** Machine-readable AI labels become mandatory across EU-operating platforms. Front-loaded, belt-and-suspenders disclosure is already the wiki default — no action needed, but worth a re-check right after the date lands.
