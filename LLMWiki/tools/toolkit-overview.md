## ⚠️ Commercial use — read this before picking any tool

**Ambassador content is commercial.** The promotional pathway can pay you DGD recognition, which
creates a material connection ([participation pathways](../dgd/participation-pathways.md)). That
means "free for personal use" tiers **do not cover you**, and this is the single most common way
to get a strike or a takedown that has nothing to do with compliance framing.

| Tool | Free tier OK for rewarded content? |
|---|---|
| **Your own voice** | ✅ always |
| **Microsoft Edge Read Aloud / Edge TTS** | ✅ |
| **CapCut** (editor + TTS) | ✅ |
| **Google Gemini / Imagen** | ✅ commercial permitted, no watermark |
| **Leonardo AI** | ✅ free plan allows commercial |
| **YouTube Audio Library · Pixabay · Uppbeat** | ✅ cleared libraries |
| **Adobe Firefly** | ⚠️ commercial allowed, but free users get **no IP indemnification** |
| **Canva free Magic Media** | ⚠️ only under Canva's content licence |
| **ElevenLabs free** | ❌ **no commercial licence**, and requires "elevenlabs.io" in the title |
| **Bing Image Creator free** | ❌ **non-commercial personal use only** |
| **Ideogram free** | ❌ commercial requires paid |
| **Trending platform audio** | ❌ licensed for personal use, not branded content |

Watermarks are a *separate* question from licensing — a watermark-free export can still be
non-commercial. Check both.

## Two honest realities about "free"

1. **Free tiers shift monthly.** Credits, watermarks, resolution caps, and even tool names change. Every tool page is tagged `verified: 2026-06`. Treat specifics as "last known," and confirm on the tool's site.
2. **Watermarks matter for cross-posting.** A watermark from one app can get your video down-ranked or rejected on another platform. Prefer no-watermark free exports where possible (noted per tool).

## Recommended "all-free" starter stack (intermediate creator)

> **Script:** ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini free → **Voice:** your own voice or Edge Read Aloud (label AI if synthetic) → **Visuals:** Veo via Gemini or Kling free + **Gemini/Imagen or Leonardo** images → **Edit & captions:** CapCut → **Music:** YouTube Audio Library / Pixabay → **Schedule:** native platform schedulers or a free Postiz/Buffer tier.

Full step-by-step: [workflows.md](workflows.md).

## Golden setup rules

- **Keep a `/raw` folder** of your generated clips, voiceovers, and images so you can reuse and re-cut. (Mirrors the wiki's raw-sources idea.)
- **Caption everything** — most short-form is watched on mute.
- **Export vertical 1080×1920** unless a platform needs otherwise. → [platform specs](../craft/platform-specs.md)
- **Disclose AI voices/avatars and your sponsorship** every time. → [AI disclosure](../compliance/ai-disclosure.md) · [FTC](../compliance/ftc-disclosure.md)

## A note on tool choice and the message
No tool excuses a bad script or a compliance slip. The [communications discipline](../compliance/communications-discipline.md) governs the *content* no matter how it's made. Great tools + safe, educational message = your edge.
